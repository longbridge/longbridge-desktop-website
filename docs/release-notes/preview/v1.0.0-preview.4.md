# v1.0.0-preview.4  <Badge type="warning" text="preview" />

_Release Date: 2026-10-22_

### New Features

- **Trade**: Added odd lot orders for Hong Kong and Singapore stocks, so you can buy or sell odd lots directly; if the quantity isn't a whole number of lots, you'll be prompted to switch to an odd lot order.
- **Trade**: Coupons are now matched to the selected trading session (such as regular hours, pre/post-market or overnight), and the coupon that saves the most on this order's estimated fees is recommended, so higher-value coupons are no longer wasted.
- **Market**: Added an earnings calendar that shows upcoming earnings releases for each market by week or month; click a stock to see its past results and market expectations, helping you prepare for earnings season.
- **Auto Update**: New versions are now downloaded and installed in the background by default, and the title bar prompts you to restart once it's done, so your work isn't interrupted; you can turn this off in "Settings > General".

### Improvements

- Risk disclosures are now checked leg by leg for multi-leg option orders, and the short sell notice shows correctly when a leg sells options; the option short sell notice now has a "Don't remind me again" option.
- Fixed an issue where, after switching accounts, the trade panel still used the previous account's order types and quantity units.
- Fixed issues including incorrect sorting and paging in the P&L ranking, Today's Statistics showing stale data after switching accounts, and holding details showing `--`.
- Fixed a crash on launch when the local database file was corrupted; the app now backs it up and recreates it automatically. Also fixed a crash when tapping the fingerprint button while Touch ID was unavailable.
